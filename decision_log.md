# Desk Switch Enclosure Dimensions

* Base Plate: 3.6 mm
* Front wall: 2.6 mm
* Side walls: 2.6 mm
* Back wall: 2.6 mm

## Base Plate Groove
* Height: 1.8 mm

## Side Wall Mounting Tongue
* Height: 1.6 mm
* Depth: 3.2 mm

## Gaps
